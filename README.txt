CHATFRFR WEBSITE TEMPLATE

Files:
- index.html: public homepage
- privacy-policy.html: separate privacy policy URL
- terms.html: separate terms of service URL

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Open privacy-policy.html and terms.html in a text editor and replace every [placeholder].
2. Make sure all privacy statements match what your app actually does.
3. Keep all three files together when uploading to your host.
4. On GitHub Pages, upload these files to the root of your repository. The URLs will be:
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPOSITORY/
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPOSITORY/privacy-policy.html
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPOSITORY/terms.html
5. Put the first URL in Google's Homepage field and the privacy-policy.html URL in its Privacy Policy field.

This template does not guarantee OAuth verification. Verify ownership of the site and accurately document the data/scopes your app uses.
